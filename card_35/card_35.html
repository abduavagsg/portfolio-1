The 35 Karuta Cards - かるたのカード

Click on any card to read its poem.

The syllables written in red are the Kimariji (決まり字): the "deciding syllables" of each poem. The moment a player hears them, he knows which card to take, even before the reader finishes the poem. Memorizing the kimariji is the secret of every Karuta champion!
